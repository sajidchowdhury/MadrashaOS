# MadrashaOS — User Guide

> Guide for end-users: teachers, guardians, and accountants.

## For Teachers

### Logging In

1. Go to `http://your-domain.org/login`
2. Enter your email and password (provided by the madrasha office)
3. You'll see the **Teacher Dashboard** with your class summary

### Taking Attendance

1. Click **Attendance** in the left sidebar, then **Take Attendance**
2. Select your class and section from the dropdowns
3. Each student defaults to **Present** — tap a student to cycle through:
   - Present (green) → Absent (red) → Late (amber) → Leave (blue) → Present
4. Use the **Present All** button to reset everyone to Present
5. Click **Submit** when done
6. You'll see a summary: "8 Present, 2 Absent"
7. If you need to undo, tap **Undo** in the toast within 30 seconds

**Tip:** The elapsed timer at the top shows how long you've spent. Aim for under 60 seconds for a 40-student class.

### Entering Exam Marks

1. Click **Examinations** in the sidebar
2. Find your exam and click **Enter Marks**
3. Enter marks for each student (use the **Next** button to advance)
4. The system validates: marks can't exceed the exam's full marks
5. If a student was absent, toggle the **Absent** button instead of entering 0
6. On the last student, click **Save & Submit**
7. You'll see a summary with the class average

### Viewing Results

1. Click **Results** in the sidebar
2. Select the class and exam
3. You'll see each student's total marks, GPA, grade, and pass/fail status

### Sending Notices to Parents

1. Click **Notices** in the sidebar
2. Click **Compose**
3. Select audience: **Guardians** (sends to parents only) or **All**
4. Write the title and body
5. Click **Send** — parents with notifications configured will receive an email/SMS

## For Guardians

### Logging In

1. Go to `http://your-domain.org/login`
2. Enter your email and password (provided by the madrasha office)
3. You'll see the **Guardian Portal** with your children's overview

### Viewing Your Child's Attendance

1. On the dashboard, click on your child's name
2. Switch to the **Attendance** tab
3. You'll see recent attendance records with present/absent counts

### Viewing Your Child's Results

1. On the dashboard, click on your child's name
2. Switch to the **Fees** tab to see fee plans and payment history
3. Results are shown under the **Results** section when published

### Viewing Fee Plans

1. On the dashboard, click on your child's name
2. Switch to the **Fees** tab
3. You'll see all installments with their status (Paid / Pending)
4. Contact the madrasha office to make a payment

### Reading Notices

1. Notices sent to "Guardians" or "All" appear on the public website
2. Visit `http://your-domain.org/public/notices` (no login required)
3. Click **Read More** on any notice to see the full text

## For Accountants

### Collecting a Fee Payment

1. Click **Fees** in the sidebar
2. Click **Collect Payment**
3. **Step 1:** Search for the student by name or code
4. **Step 2:** Select the outstanding installment, enter the amount received, choose payment method (cash/bank/mobile), select the receiving account
5. **Step 3:** Preview the receipt → click **Confirm Payment**
6. The system generates a receipt number (e.g. `RCP-2026-1009`)
7. A ledger entry is posted automatically (debit Cash, credit Fee Income)

### Posting a Ledger Entry

1. Click **Accounting** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Entry**
3. Select debit account + credit account (must be different)
4. Enter amount + narration
5. Click **Post** — the entry is balanced automatically

### Receiving a Donation

1. Public donations arrive automatically via the public website
2. To record a manual donation: navigate to **Donations** → **Add Donation**
3. Enter donor name, amount, type (general/zakat/sadaqah)
4. Zakat donations are automatically routed to the isolated Zakat fund

### Distributing Zakat

1. Click **Zakat** in the sidebar
2. Click **Distribute**
3. Select the beneficiary + amount
4. The system debits the Zakat fund and credits the beneficiary

### Viewing the Ledger

1. Click **Accounting** in the sidebar
2. The Ledger Explorer shows all entries with running balance
3. Filter by account, date range, or fund type
4. Click any entry to see the full voucher details

## Language Switching

At any time, use the language buttons in the top bar:
- **EN** — English
- **বাং** — Bangla (Bengali)
- **ع** — Arabic (RTL layout)

The entire interface switches instantly, including dates and numbers.
